# Henry Home Automation Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 24, 2026**

Henry Home Automation is a private, family-use home and RV automation system. It is not a commercial messaging service and is not available for public registration.

## Information We Collect

The system may store the mobile phone numbers of household or family members who have chosen to receive automated notifications.

The system may also process information generated by the home and RV automation systems, such as equipment status, water leak events, power failures, system faults, and weather alerts.

## How Information Is Used

Mobile phone numbers are used only to deliver operational, safety, and system-related notifications requested by participating household or family members.

Examples may include:

- Water leak or water-system alerts
- Power failure notifications
- Home or RV system faults
- Equipment or automation alerts
- Active severe weather alerts affecting the home, RV, or travel route

No advertising or promotional messages are sent.

## Mobile Information and Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, messaging opt-in information, and consent records are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be processed by communications service providers when necessary to deliver SMS or voice notifications.

## Consent

Recipients are added only after they have directly agreed to receive automated notifications from Henry Home Automation.

There is no public signup process.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on system events. Most recipients should receive messages only when relevant alerts or events occur.

Message and data rates may apply.

## Opting Out

Recipients may opt out at any time by replying **STOP** to an SMS message or by asking the system owner to remove their phone number.

Recipients may reply **HELP** for assistance.

## Data Retention

Phone numbers and related configuration information are retained only while the recipient participates in the family notification system.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the home automation system or messaging features change.

## Contact

This service is operated privately for household and family use. Recipients may contact the system owner directly for questions or support.
